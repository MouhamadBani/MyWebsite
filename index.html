I am a doctoral graduate research assistant at North Dakota State University, pursuing a PhD in Transportation and Supply Chain Management. My work covers study design, statistical analysis, and data visualization, using a variety of statistical tools, software, and methodologies. I enjoy applying machine learning techniques to uncover patterns and working with GIS to turn complex data into clear, actionable insights that support strategic decisions and optimize processes.
